    A toolkit for teaching, learning, and doing research in Boolean function analysis: Fourier analysis, property testing, query complexity, hypercontractivity, pseudorandomness. 25 notebooks aligned to O'Donnell's Analysis of Boolean Functions, cross-validated against SageMath and published values.
